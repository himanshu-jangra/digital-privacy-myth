# Digital Privacy is a Myth*

> A wake-up call for the digital age. This is the official home for the book by **Himanshu Jangra**.

## 📖 About the Book

In an era where every click, search, and swipe is tracked, stored, and monetised, **Digital Privacy is a Myth\*** pulls back the curtain on the invisible machinery of surveillance capitalism. 

This book is not just an exploration of the problems, it's a manual for resistance. It challenges the dangerous comfort of "I have nothing to hide" and reveals why privacy is fundamentally about power and control. Whether you're a tech professional or a casual internet user, this book provides the tools and knowledge to claw back your digital autonomy.

### What's Inside:
- **The Illusion of Privacy:** Why current "protections" often fail.
- **The Data Harvesting Machine:** How your identity is traded like a commodity.
- **The Regulatory Maze:** Navigating laws like GDPR and CCPA.
- **Taking Back Control:** Practical toolkits for encrypted communications and digital hygiene.
