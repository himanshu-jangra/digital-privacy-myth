Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. A book about digital privacy should practice what it preaches.

What We Collect (and Why)

If you voluntarily submit the download form or feedback form, you may provide: your name, email address, and phone/WhatsApp number. This information is used solely to read your feedback and, if requested, to notify you about the audiobook launch. It is never sold, never shared, and never used for marketing.

If you join the audiobook waitlist, we collect your email address only. It will be used exclusively to notify you when the audiobook launches nothing else.

When you download the free PDF, we log the download event whether you submitted the form or skipped it. This log records only the download method (form or skip) and a timestamp. No personal information is attached unless you voluntarily provided it via the form. This helps us understand how many readers are downloading the book, nothing more.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We use no cookies, not even "necessary" ones.
  • We use no analytics scripts, no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no Plausible, no Fathom, nothing.
  • We log no IP addresses.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not fingerprint your browser.
  • There are no tracking pixels anywhere on this site.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing jangrahimanshu@protonmail.com. We will comply promptly because that's how it should work.
